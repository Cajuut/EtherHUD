EtherHUD is a client-side HUD mod for **Minecraft 1.21.4 on Fabric**. Pick the information you want, place it where it fits, and customize it in-game. Press **Right Shift** to open EtherHUD Settings.

## Make the HUD yours

- **10 modules** — FPS, Coordinates, CPS, Armor Status, Ping, TPS, Player Model, Item Total, Compass, and Image Overlay. TPS is estimated from server time updates.
- **HUD Editor** — Move or resize modules, select several at once, and use snapping, a grid, and undo. Press Esc to save and close the editor.
- **Appearance controls** — Use a color picker to customize text, backgrounds, and outlines. Adjust opacity, shadows, corner radius, and scale with a live preview.
- **Armor Status** — Show held items and armor with durability indicators and low-durability alerts. Choose spaced slots, a joined strip, or a vanilla hotbar style.
- **Image Overlay** — Display a local image or animated GIF as a HUD module.
- **First-use tips** — Short prompts point to the controls while you use them. Replay the guide from **More → Replay tips**.

## Customize game overlays

Open **More → Overlay** to adjust the Totem of Undying animation’s size, position, and speed. You can also adjust how high the first-person fire overlay sits, the size of fire and soul fire in the world, the size and position of items held in either hand, and shield opacity.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.4**, **Java 21**, and **Fabric Loader 0.16.14 or newer**
- **Fabric API 0.119.2 or newer** for Minecraft 1.21.4
- Mod Menu and Cloth Config are optional

The interface is available in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean. Module names are shown in English in every language.
